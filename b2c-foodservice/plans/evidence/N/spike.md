## Other findings from the documentation pass

1. **Early orders and renewals**: orders created by the schedule do not emit `OrderCreated`, only `OrderCreatedFromRecurringOrder` (so a connector that listens to `OrderCreated` misses renewals). Not relevant to the storefront, noted for any later integration.
2. **Payment of renewals**: without Checkout recurring payments (public beta, Q-N-5/OA-N1) the platform creates the order and "any financial transaction is handled by your external PSP integration". The storefront does nothing for renewals.
3. **A failed renewal** moves the recurring order to `Failed` and it does not retry: the card shows "Needs attention", the contact link and Cancel (no "set back to Active" by the shopper, because the cause (stock, configuration) is the shop's).
4. **Inventory mode** matters for renewals (`ReserveOnOrder` fails the order when stock is short).
5. `request order now` (`requestOrderNow`) exists; the storefront does not offer it (not in the design).
