# Questions: workstream V (preset bundles)

Draft for the lead to consolidate into `QUESTIONS.md`.

| ID | Question | Default | Blocks? | Status | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | 
| Q-V-1 | How is the preset discount carried in the cart? | **A:** the bundle (parent) line carries `round(sum of the parts × (1 − presetDiscount))` as `externalPrice`; the machine and pods are $0 child lines (commerce-patterns `bundle-modeling.md`). The bag row total is the engine's own line total. Alternative **B:** the parts carry their discounted prices and the parent is $0 (per-SKU revenue and tax stay exact, but the bundle total becomes a display sum and is rounded per part). | No | Answered | A (owner, 2026-11-16). |
| Q-V-2 | `presetDiscount` is a Number. Is `0.1` 10 % or 0.1 %? | A fraction: `0.1` = 10 %. Values `< 0`, `>= 1` or not a number hide the preset (logged). | No | Answered | 10 % (a fraction: 0.1 = 10 %). |
| Q-V-3 | Presets have no id. How does the bag remember which preset was chosen? | By position in `presets[]` (`bundlePreset = "0"`). The price is locked on the line, so reordering presets in Merchant Center changes nothing already in a bag except on a market switch or re-price, which re-reads by position. | No | Answered | Default. |
| Q-V-4 | A machine has colour variants. Which one goes into the bundle? | The machine's master variant (Vertuo Studio black, Pod Plus Milk white).
