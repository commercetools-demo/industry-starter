## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Brand switcher above the header
The shell SHALL render the brand switcher tabs above the promise bar and header, and SHALL scope navigation and the promise bar to the current brand (no collection promise or store pill on Malva Drinks).

#### Scenario: Shell on drinks
- **WHEN** a Malva Drinks page renders in the US market
- **THEN** the switcher is the topmost element and the store pill is absent
