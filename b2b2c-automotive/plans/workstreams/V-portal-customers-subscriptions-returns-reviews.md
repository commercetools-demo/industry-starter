# Workstream V — Portal customers: shoppers, shop as customer, subscriptions, returns and reviews

Status: DRAFT v2 (2026-10-12, owner answers folded in). Main work in `b2b2c-automotive/portal/`; the small, listed changes in `b2b2c-automotive/storefront/` are cross-cutting and marked `[STOREFRONT]`. Dev URLs assumed: portal `http://localhost:3001`, storefront `http://localhost:3000` (use the ports workstream A set).

Origin: this workstream is the first half of the former "W" (customers, returns, marketing, bundles, brand). The former wholesale workstream V (buying from Malva) is **out of scope** (owner, Q-V01, 2026-10-12) and was deleted; its letter now belongs to this workstream. Marketing, email, bundles, import and brand partnership are workstream W.

## Goal

A reseller's staff look after **their own shoppers**: see them (and nobody else's), act for a shopper safely, **manage subscriptions** (pause, resume, cancel, skip), handle returns including the **refund through the commercetools Checkout and the Stripe connector**, and moderate reviews. Everything runs on the signed-in reseller's store on the server (`authorizeStore`), never from a request value.
