# Workstream W — Portal selling tools: marketing and email (stored, never sent), bundles, product import, brand partnership

Status: DRAFT v2 (2026-10-12, owner answers folded in). Main work in `b2b2c-automotive/portal/`; the small, listed changes in `b2b2c-automotive/storefront/` are cross-cutting and marked `[STOREFRONT]`. Dev URLs assumed: portal `http://localhost:3001`, storefront `http://localhost:3000` (use the ports workstream A set).

Origin: this is the second half of the former "W". Customers, shop as customer, subscriptions, returns and reviews moved to workstream V. The former wholesale workstream V (buying from Malva) is out of scope and was deleted.

## Goal

A reseller's staff use Malva's co-op campaigns, prepare their own **e-mail templates and campaigns (stored and previewed, never sent)**, sell **bundles** and **local products**, import products from CSV, and see the remaining brand-partnership guardrail (local fitment claims) with what to fix. Everything a seller does is scoped to the signed-in reseller's store on the server (`authorizeStore`); the server, not the screens, enforces it.
