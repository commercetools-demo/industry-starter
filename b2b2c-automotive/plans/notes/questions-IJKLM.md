Updated 2026-10-12 after the owner interview (`notes/owner-answers.md`, binding). Answered questions are in the first table with the answer the workstream files now use; open questions keep the default their plan takes. Build on the default; if the owner answers differently, only the item in the last column changes.

| Id | Question | Answer (binding) | Workstream files updated |

| Q-I01 | Branches as supply channels on one store, or separate stores? | **One store per reseller; branches are channels on that store that carry both inventory and prices** (owner answer Q-005). | Q-J03 | Fitment as explicit vehicle ids or as ranges? | **Year ranges** (make, model, year-from, year-to, engine; owner answer Q-J03).
