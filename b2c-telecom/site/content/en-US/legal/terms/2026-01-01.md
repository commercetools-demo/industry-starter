## Who can order
You must be able to enter into a contract in your country to order from Malva Telecom. Some plans depend on your address or on your eligibility, and the plan page tells you when that applies.

## Plans and billing
Internet and phone plans are monthly services and are billed every month once service starts. Each plan shows its contract term before you order. Introductory prices end as shown in My bundle, and the price that follows is shown before you pay.

## Ending a contract early
If you end a plan before its contract term finishes, an early termination fee may apply. The fee, if any, is shown on the Broadband Facts label of the plan.

## Equipment and phones
Equipment and phones are covered by the [Shipping and returns policy](/legal/shipping-returns).
