## What we store
If you create an account, we store your name, email address, saved addresses and your orders so that you can see and manage them. If you do not create an account, we keep your bundle only for as long as your browser session lasts.

## Cookies
We use one session cookie to keep your bundle and your sign-in, and one cookie to remember your language and region. We do not set cookies for advertising.

## What we do not do
We do not send marketing email and we do not use analytics or tracking scripts on this site.

## Your choices
To ask for your data to be corrected or deleted, email [support](/support) from the address on your account. We will confirm when the request is done.
