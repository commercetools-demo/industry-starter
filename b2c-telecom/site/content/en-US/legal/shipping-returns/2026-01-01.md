## Shipping
Equipment and phones ship to the delivery address you choose at checkout. Digital items such as streaming add-ons need no shipping and are available once your service starts. My bundle and your order page show what ships and what does not.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order from your order page until your service start date. After that date, the terms of your plan apply.

## Returning a phone
You can request a phone return from your order page within the return period shown there. Keep the phone in its original condition and packaging. We will confirm the return on your order page.
