## Shipping
Equipment that you order ships to the delivery address you give at checkout. Digital items such as streaming add-ons need no shipping and are available once your service starts. Delivery for equipment is arranged after your order is placed, and we will show the status on your order page.

## Returns
Returns are accepted on request for unused equipment. To ask for a return, contact [support](/support) and give your order number. Opened or used equipment may be refused. We will tell you how to send the item back and will confirm the return once it arrives.
