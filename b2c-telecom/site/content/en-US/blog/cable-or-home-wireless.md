Choosing between cable and home wireless internet starts with how each one reaches your home.

## What each one is
Cable internet arrives over a wired connection that runs to your address. Home wireless internet uses a mobile network: a receiver in your home picks up the signal and shares it over Wi-Fi.

## Setting up
Cable internet needs an installation visit, so there is a short wait before your service starts. Home wireless internet arrives as a receiver that you plug in yourself, which means you can usually start sooner and take it with you if you move.

## When a wired connection fits
A wired connection suits homes where many devices are online at once, or where steady speeds matter for work, video calls or gaming. Home wireless suits places where a cable is hard to install, or where you want a simple set-up without a visit.
