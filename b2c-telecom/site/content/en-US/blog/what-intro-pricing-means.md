Some plans start with a lower price, or with a free period, and then move to the regular price. We call the lower price an introductory price.

## When it starts
An introductory period starts on the day you place your order. It does not wait for your service start date, and it does not change the time you have to cancel an order. Those are separate: you can cancel from your order page until your service start date.

## What you see before you order
My bundle shows the whole schedule for everything you picked: the introductory price, how long it lasts, and the price that follows. You never have to guess what the next price will be, because it is on screen before you pay.

## Several items, one schedule
If your bundle holds an internet plan and a phone plan, each one can have its own introductory period. My bundle lines them up so you can see the total for each stage.

## After you order
The same schedule is on your order page. If you want to compare it with the plan itself, open the plan and read its Broadband Facts label, which is explained in [how to read the label](/blog/reading-the-broadband-facts-label). For other questions, see the [FAQ](/faq).
