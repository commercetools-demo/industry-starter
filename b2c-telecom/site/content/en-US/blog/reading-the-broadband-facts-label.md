Every internet plan at Malva Telecom comes with a Broadband Facts label. It is a standard summary, laid out the same way for every plan, so you can compare them without hunting through small print.

## What is on it
The label starts with the monthly price. If the plan has an introductory price, the label shows it and the price that follows. Below that you find the fees that may apply, such as an equipment fee or a charge for ending the contract early, and the typical download and upload speeds.

## Comparing two plans
Put two labels side by side and read them in the same order: monthly price, price after any introduction, fees, then speeds. A lower first price is not always the lower price over the whole term, so look at both lines before you decide.

## Where to find it
Open any internet plan in the [shop](/shop/cable-internet) and select the plan to see its label. My bundle repeats the schedule for everything you have picked, so the price you see at the end matches the label.
