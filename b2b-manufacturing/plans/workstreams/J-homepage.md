## Design
`design/malva/specs/homepage.md`. Static/ISR page, identical for every visitor (D20). Sections in order: hero · two service pillars · stats (dark) · audiences · accreditations + testimonials (wash) · CTA band.
- Hero: full-bleed photo from `content/images` slot `home-hero` (clean URL, `next/image` with `priority`, explicit sizes, overlay 40 % black), H1 "Plumbing and waste management for sites that can't stop.", lead, buttons **Request a quote** (white) and **Explore services** (outline white).
- Pillars: two `ServiceCard`s linking to the listings (images slots `pillar-plumbing`, `pillar-waste`).
- Audiences: four `AudienceCard`s; each is a link to `/request-a-quote?sector=<key>` (spec scenario "Audience card opens a prefilled quote").
- Metadata: unique `<title>` and description, canonical, Open Graph image (`opengraph-image.tsx`), one `<h1>`.
- Images come from `site/content/images.ts` which reads `seed/data/site-images.json` copied at build time by `scripts/sync-site-images.mjs` (clean URLs; a test fails on `?` or `#`).
