## Design
Data decision D5/Q-020: demo data stored as Custom Objects behind `PortalDataSource` (defined in O). Read-only in v1; the screens are real, the data is seeded and labelled "Demo data" in the portal footer.
- `lib/portal/custom-object-source.ts` implements `PortalDataSource` over containers `mpw-visits`, `mpw-waste-docs`, `mpw-invoices`, keys `<businessUnitKey>.<id>`; queries by key prefix `where=key like "<bu>.*"` restricted to the session's Business Unit (**never** a client-supplied key), mapped to `Visit`, `WasteDocument`, `Invoice` app types.
- **Service visits** table: date (DD/MM/YYYY) · site · service · status badge (Scheduled / In progress / Completed / Missed, text + style) · report download; filters by site and status, reflected in the URL (`?site=&status=`).
- **Waste documents**: list of transfer/consignment notes and annual reports by site, waste type, date; download. **Diversion rate**: monthly figure and trailing 12 months with the weights it is computed from (recycled kg / total kg).
- **Invoices**: number · date · site · amount (the only other place money is shown, with `formatMoney`) · status (Paid / Due / Overdue, text + style) · PDF download; overdue shows how to contact accounts; **no online payment**.
- Downloads (Q-021): `GET /api/portal/documents/[type]/[id]` renders a simple PDF on the fly with `pdf-lib` from the stored record, after `requireOwnership`; file name `<type>-<number>-<yyyy-mm-dd>.pdf`; `Content-Disposition: attachment`; `no-store`. Another company's id → 404.
- Alerts for the overview: expiring certificate (`alerts` in the data) and missed collections.
- Seed: workstream E created the demo data (12 visits, 8 waste notes, 6 invoices for `mpw-demo-co`, different for `mpw-other-co`).
