# Workstream L — Service detail pages (PDP)

**Depends on:** K
**Implements:** malva-service-detail[Each service has a detail page; Related services; Search-engine and sharing metadata]
**Skills:** commercetools-storefront (`b2b/product-detail.md`, `core/product-detail.md`)

## Design
`design/malva/specs/pdp.md` (proposed, SO-03 for content). Route `/[locale]/<category>/<slug>` with `generateStaticParams` from the catalog; static/ISR, no price anywhere (D21).
- Header: breadcrumb `Home / Plumbing / <name>`, H1, lead = card description.
- Body `2fr / 1fr`: photo · What's included (check list) · Who it's for (`Tag`s from `sectors`) · How it works (numbered steps) · Compliance and records (`records`) · FAQ accordion (native `<details>`/`<summary>`, keyboard operable) · Related services (≤ 3 `ServiceCard`s, never the current one, none if no relation).
- Right sticky card: **Request a quote for this service** (link to `/request-a-quote?service=<slug>`; built in Q), **Add to quote list** (island, built in P — render a disabled placeholder with the final markup until P), phone, response promise, emergency note. < 900 px: the card moves above the long content and a sticky bottom bar repeats the primary action.
- Structured data JSON-LD: `Service` (name, description, provider Malva, serviceType, areaServed US or DE per locale) and `BreadcrumbList` matching the visible trail; canonical URL; unique title/description; OG image.
- Unknown or unpublished slug → `notFound()` with links to both listings.
