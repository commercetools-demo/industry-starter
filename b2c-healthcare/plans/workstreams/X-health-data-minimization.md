## Design
Rules were applied from D onward; X **proves** them and adds the operations. Boundary (D-025): what crosses into commerce is an identifier, a quantity and a price — orders carry `rxNumber`, `rxLineRef`, quantity, price, never sig/diagnosis/lab values/reason; the demo clinical stand-in (Custom Objects) is isolated in containers `malva-rx|lab|credential|booking` and is **enumerated** in `docs/privacy-inventory.md` (every resource and custom object container that may hold personal data).
Operations (scripts in `site/scripts/privacy/`, run with the admin seed credentials, with `--dry-run`):
- `erase-patient.ts <customerId|email>`: uses **`dataErasure=true`** on DELETE for Customer, Cart, Order, Payment, Review, ShoppingList, CustomObject (bookings, rx, labs, credentials, allowances, refill log, rate limits, recurring orders where applicable) — plain DELETE is never used; reports every deleted id.
- `subject-access.ts <customerId>`: queries **all** of Customer, Cart, Order, Payment, Review, ShoppingList, DiscountCode, CustomObject (all `malva-*` containers by `patientRef`), Message, BusinessUnit, Quote, QuoteRequest, StagedQuote by customer id predicate and writes a JSON report (to stdout/file outside the repo).
- `retention.ts`: removes/de-identifies guest bookings past `expiresAt` (90 days after the visit), rate-limit objects, stale refill logs; schedulable as a Netlify function.
- Test-environment guard: `seed.ts` refuses to import anything not synthetic (`example.com` emails, `555` phones allow-list) and a test asserts no production-looking data in `data/`.
- Disclosure through the goods: confirmation page, order list and any (future) packing slip show **medication names** (decision to confirm at SO-04) — documented in `docs/privacy-inventory.md`; there are no emails/labels in v1.
- Messages are disabled in the project (PROJECT-FINDINGS); keep it so; a check in `seed:verify` fails if enabled.
