## Design
`/[locale]/doctor/[key]?m=remote|office`: breadcrumb "← All doctors" (restores the list's mode and filters via a `back` query param carrying the list URL, sanitized), two columns (content; 380 px sticky booking panel). Content: header card (104 px avatar/portrait, H1, specialty, badges "★ rating · N reviews", "N yrs experience"), About (bio; Education, Languages, Clinic), Patient reviews (only verified reviews, "Verified patient · Mon YYYY"; omitted when none). Unknown key → **HTTP 404** "Doctor not found. Back to search". Product detail rules: the fee shown is the price the visitor would pay for the chosen mode; "no price resolves" shows "Fee unavailable" and disables booking; a mode the doctor does not offer is disabled.
Booking panel (client, data uncached from `GET /api/doctors/:key/slots?mode=`): mode toggle (fee updates), 7-day picker (selected = navy-700), 3-column slot grid, "No times left on this day. Try another date.", footer "Booking as <name>" / "No account needed. You can book as a guest." Slot click opens the **Confirm booking modal**: summary (doctor, mode, date, time, fee), guest fields Full name, Email, Phone, Reason for visit (all required) + consent/retention line (SO-04, Q-025) or signed-in "Booking as <name> (<email>)" with Phone + Reason only; "Booking as a guest. Sign in instead" link. Close = overlay/Close/Escape, focus returns to the slot, focus trapped.
`POST /api/bookings` → `lib/ct/bookings.createBooking` (F-03): claims the slot (version-0 custom object). Slot taken → 409 → modal says the time is no longer available and the grid refetches. Success → redirect `/booked/<ref>`. **Reason text is health data**: never logged, never in URLs/analytics/order notes; stored only in the booking object; guests' bookings carry `expiresAt = visit + 90 days` (Q-025). No emails are sent (D-029/Q-071): confirmation copy "Keep your reference <ref>. It is also under My appointments." (guest nudge to create an account).
`/booked/[ref]`: green "Booking confirmed" badge, H1 "You're booked, <first name>.", rows Reference, Doctor, When, Type, Join/Where, Fee "$N · pay at the visit"; guest nudge "Create an account" / signed-in "My appointments". Access: the booking's own session (signed-in patient who owns it, or the guest session that created it via an HTTP-only `malva_bk` cookie holding a signed list of refs) — anyone else sees a plain not-found. Attaching guest bookings to a new account by verified-email match is done in R-07.
