# Workstream V — Static content: FAQ, contact, about, policies, journal

**Depends on:** C, G, H
**Implements:** faq, contact-us, about-us, policy-pages, blog-resources
**Skills:** commercetools-storefront

## Design
No CMS exists, so content is **files in the repo** under `site/content/` (Markdown with front matter) read at build time; all pages are static (SSG) and never call commercetools, so they survive a degraded commerce tier (about-us). Copy is drafted by the developer as clearly marked **draft** placeholder text (Q-052); legal wording needs SO-03/SO-04. Design not provided → SO-03. Pages:
- **FAQ** `/faq`: topic headings (Booking, Prescriptions & delivery, Lab results, Account & privacy); every answer's full text is in the initial HTML (native `<details>` is allowed only if the answer text is in the DOM; deep links `/faq#question-id` open and scroll to it); feedback ("Was this helpful?") collector is **omitted** in v1 so "collector unreachable" cannot fail (scenario N/A with that reason); untranslated answer falls back to en-US text (only en-US exists).
- **Contact** `/contact` (Q-072): support email, phone, hours and office addresses as plain text; **no form** and **no chat script** (scenarios "Enquiry accepted downstream"/"Routing unavailable"/"Chat script absent" map to N/A; "Region without an office" shows the general contact block). Emergency disclaimer present.
- **About** `/about`: company story, credentials, route to sales/partnership contact; assembled only from content files; a correction published = rebuild; locale without translation shows en-US.
- **Policies** `/policies/shipping-and-returns`, `/policies/terms`, `/policies/privacy`: each states "Effective <date>" from front matter and keeps superseded versions as `content/policies/<slug>/<date>.md` with `/policies/<slug>?version=<date>` access; opened from checkout via link; the privacy page describes data minimization (what is kept where, retention of guest bookings 90 days).
- **Journal** `/journal` and `/journal/[slug]` (Q-050): 3 seeded articles (title, description, category, minutes, cover from `site-images.json` `journal-1..3`); each article at its own URL with title/description/canonical metadata; list filter by category with "no articles match" state and a withdrawn article (front matter `withdrawn: true`) → 410-style "no longer available" page with link back; article without tags OK. Home journal row (M-07) shows when ≥ 3 published articles exist.
Footer/nav links added in H footers point here (H-08 had omitted undesigned links; this workstream turns them on via the route manifest).
