## Design
Components in `components/ui` (primitives) and `components/layout` (shell), styled only through tokens, every interactive element a real `a`/`button` with the global focus ring (B-06). Source: `design/DESIGN.md` component table, `design/source/app.css`, `app-core.jsx` (`Nav`, `Foot`, `Head`).
Primitives: `Button` (primary azure fill + `--color-action-label`, outline, small, full, `busy`/disabled state), `Badge` (ok/wait/info/no/neutral using `*-700` text tokens), `Card`, `Field`/`Input`/`Select`/`Textarea` (filled neutral-25, no `outline:0`), `SegmentedControl` (links or buttons), `Avatar` (initials on peach or `src` portrait), `PageHead` (sky gradient), `Modal` (overlay, 520px card, Escape closes, focus trapped, focus returns to opener), `Toast` + `LiveRegion` (bottom-center navy, 5 s, announces to screen readers), `Pagination`, `Skeleton`, `EmptyState`, `StatusTimeline`.
Shell: `Header` (sticky 72 px, white 95% + blur): logo (links to `/en-US`, Q-021), links Remote sessions / Office visits / Prescriptions / Lab tests with active-section mapping (`/doctors/remote`; `/doctors/office`; `/prescriptions`+`/cart`+`/checkout`+`/order/*`; `/account/labs`); Cart button with count bubble = **number of lines** (Q-020); account slot = "Sign in" (anonymous) or 36 px initials avatar linking to `/account`; **cart count and identity come from session-resolved SWR data, never from cached markup**. Home variant (adds Home and Health journal when articles exist, "Sign in" outline + "Book a visit"). Mobile (< 900 px): links collapse into a slide-down drawer (Q-022, SO-02). `Footer` (app: "© 2026 Malva Healthcare" + "Not for emergencies — call your local emergency number."; home: brand blurb + Care / Pharmacy / Company columns, only links to real pages). `RequireSignIn` card ("Sign in to view your cart." etc., reasons from the spec) that returns to the route after sign-in (`?next=` validated as a same-origin locale path).
Smoke page `/[locale]` proves routing + messages + tokens + commercetools connection (calls `getProjectSettings`).
