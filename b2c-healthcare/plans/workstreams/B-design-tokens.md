## Design
Source of truth: `design/source/_ds/tokens.css` (84 custom properties). The storefront carries them **verbatim** in `site/app/tokens.css` imported by `globals.css`, plus an `@theme inline` block mapping them to Tailwind v4 theme variables. The token file's runtime Google-Fonts `@import` is **not** copied; fonts load through `next/font` (Poppins 400–700, Lato 400/700, Roboto 400/500, self-hosted) and are exposed as `--font-display`, `--font-meta`, `--font-body`. Departures from the design go only in a clearly marked block **"storefront extensions"** at the bottom of `app/tokens.css`: `--color-success-700`, `--color-warning-700`, `--color-info-700`, `--color-danger-700` (the prototype's #067a05, #8a5d00, #0a6f8c, #b3402a; D-009), `--container-content: 1200px` (D-008), `--color-action-label` = `--color-navy-900` (D-010, SO-01) and `--focus-ring`. The parity check ignores that block and the font `@import`.
Contrast: unit-test the ratios with a small WCAG function (white on #2aa7ff ≈ 2.6:1 fails; navy-900 on #2aa7ff must pass 4.5:1; every `*-700` text on its `-50` background ≥ 4.5:1).
Design lint: copy `design/source/_ds/_adherence.oxlintrc.json` rules verbatim into `site/.oxlintrc.json`, with the token allow-list **generated** from `tokens.css` by a script (so it never drifts).
