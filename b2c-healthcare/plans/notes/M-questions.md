# Workstream M: questions and the defaults chosen

| # | Question | Default chosen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What does "doctors available now" (hero chip, statistics band) mean? Bookings need a 2 hour lead (`MIN_LEAD_MS`), so nobody is bookable "now". | Doctors with a bookable slot today in the clinic time zone (same definition as the "Available today" badge and filter, K-questions 5). The chip reads "N doctors available today" and the band label "Doctors available today". Hidden when 0 or when the source is down. |
| 2 | Which doctors does the "available today" section show, and in which mode? | Up to 3, from the union of the remote and office lists, soonest slot first. One entry per doctor; a slot today beats a later one, video wins a tie, otherwise the sooner slot decides. The card shows that mode's fee ("Video · $35.00", "In office · $60.00") and links to `/doctor/<product key>?m=<mode>` (the link shape K's cards use; the page is workstream L). No slot today: the same cards with "Next: Tue 13" and a different sub line. No slot in 7 days: section hidden. |
| 3 | Caching of the live parts. | One shared `unstable_cache` read (60 s) of both lists for cards, chip and band (`getHomeSnapshot`). The data is public (no patient data). If the read throws (no credentials, outage) the live sections are left out and the static page still renders. |
| 4 | Statistics that exist. | Doctor count, review-weighted average rating (from each doctor's `reviewRatingStatistics` via the card), doctors available today. "2M+ consultations" and "15 min median wait" removed (Q-016). The prototype's "8,000 verified doctors" became "Doctors on Malva" with the real count (no "verified" claim is sourced). Each figure is dropped when null; the band is dropped when none is left. |
| 5 | Same-day claim. | Shown only when `getShippingMethods()` (60 s cache) contains an active method whose key ends in `same-day` with at least one zone rate (the seed key is `mlv-same-day`). Copy is "Same-day delivery in selected states" because the seed limits it to NY/TX/IL (D-033), so "in most cities" would overclaim. A failed shipping read means "no same-day". |
| 6 | Auto-refill claim flag. | `autoRefillEnabled()` in `lib/features.ts`, env `AUTO_REFILL_ENABLED=true`; defaults to FALSE until workstream T ships (documented in `.env.example`). |
| 7 | The prescription block needs a non-empty checklist even when both gated claims are off. | Two always-true items: "Look up your prescription by RX number" and "Delivery to your door". The pharmacist chat item is removed (no such feature). |
| 8 | Hero search empty submit. | `SearchForm` got an additive `required` prop: the input is `required` with pattern `.*\S.*`, so an empty or blank submit is stopped by the browser and the visitor stays on the page (works without JS). Non-empty text goes to `/<locale>/search?q=`. |
| 9 | Hero chips. | General practice, Dermatology, Pediatrics (Mental health hidden, D-018), each a link to `/doctors/remote?specialty=<key>` (the chip keys are the doctor specialty enum keys). |
| 10 | Service copy. | Rewritten where the prototype promised something the service does not do: no "by video or chat, within 15 minutes", no "sent straight to your pharmacy", no "refill reminders", no "book tests at home". Lab tests card says "Read your lab results in your account" (view-only, D-018). |
| 11 | Images. | Slots `home-hero`, `home-rx-delivery` and the journal covers come from `content/images.ts` (typed slot list kept in step with the seed's `SITE_SLOTS` by a test). `site-images.json` is `{}`, so every image area is a token gradient placeholder. A URL with `?` or `#` is rejected (D-006). The `home-cta` slot is typed but unused: the closing band is the brand gradient as in the prototype, not a photo. |
| 12 | Header search control (K-missed gap, D-017). | Added a small `components/layout/SearchLink.tsx` (a magnifier link to `/search`, whose `SearchForm` is the real form) at the right of the header, from the 900 px breakpoint up; wired in `HeaderClient.tsx` (one import and one element). A link, not an inline field, to avoid crowding the 72 px bar and to keep H's tab-order test (`Remote ... Lab tests, Cart, Sign in`) valid. Under 900 px it is hidden (the hero and `/search` carry the form). |
| 13 | Signed-in visitor on the home page (browser recipe: "Sam signed in -> header shows initials/count"). H's home header variant always showed "Sign in", even for a signed-in patient. | Small H-file change: `HomeAccountSlot` (client island using `useAccount`) renders "Sign in" (outline) for anonymous/expired and Cart (line count, hidden under 640 px) plus the initials link when signed in; `MobileMenu` home variant now shows Cart and Account (not "Sign in") for a signed-in user. Anonymous output is unchanged (H's tests still pass). |
| 14 | Shared files touched. | `messages/en-US.json` (+`home`), `.env.example` (+`AUTO_REFILL_ENABLED`), `lib/ct/doctors.ts` (extracted `listDoctors` = the unpaged part of `searchDoctors`, behaviour unchanged), `components/search/SearchForm.tsx` (+`required`), `components/layout/HeaderClient.tsx` and `MobileMenu.tsx` (items 12-13), `app/[locale]/smoke.test.tsx` (home placeholder tests removed; token sheet tests kept). `package.json`, `lib/types.ts`, `lib/nav.ts` untouched. |
| 15 | Metadata. | `generateMetadata` via `pageMetadata`: title, description (no numbers or emergency claims), canonical `<SITE_URL>/en-US`, hreflang for each supported locale plus x-default. The sitemap already lists `/`. |
