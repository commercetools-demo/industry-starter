## Delivery

- **Standard delivery** takes 1-2 business days.
- **Same-day delivery** is available for orders placed before the cut-off shown at checkout.
- Delivery fees are shown at checkout before you place the order.

## Returns

Medicine cannot be returned once it has left the pharmacy, unless it was dispensed in error or arrived damaged. If something is wrong with your order, [contact us](/contact) and we will help.

## Late or missing orders

If your order has not arrived by the date shown on the order page, contact us with your order number.
