## Using the service

Malva Healthcare lets you book remote and office consultations, order prescribed medicine and view your lab results. You must give accurate information and keep your sign-in details private.

## Bookings and orders

Prices and fees are shown before you confirm. Orders for prescribed medicine are fulfilled only against a valid prescription.

## Emergencies

The service is not for emergencies. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
