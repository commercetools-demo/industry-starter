## What we keep, and where

We keep only what we need to provide care and deliver your orders.

- **Account details** (name, email, phone) are kept in your account until you delete it.
- **Health information** such as prescriptions and lab results is shown to you after you sign in. It is never placed in web addresses, logs or analytics.
- **Orders** keep the delivery address and the items needed to fulfil and support the order. Order notes never contain health details.

## Guest bookings

If you book as a guest, we keep the booking for **90 days** so we can help you with it, then delete it.

## Your choices

You can ask us to export or delete your data. [Contact us](/contact) to do so.
