## Delivery slots

You choose a delivery slot at checkout. Slots are held for a short time while you complete your order.

## Delivery fees

Delivery fees, if any, are shown in your bag before you pay.

## Chilled items

Chilled and frozen items are packed to stay cold until you are home.
