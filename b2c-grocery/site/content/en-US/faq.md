## Ordering

### How do I place an order?
Add items to your bag, choose a delivery slot and check out. You can change your bag at any time before you pay.

### Can I change my order after paying?
Contact us as soon as you can and we will do our best to help before packing begins.

## Delivery

### Where do you deliver?
We deliver to addresses in our delivery areas. Enter your address at checkout to see if we reach you.

### What if I am not home?
Our drivers can leave chilled items in a safe place you choose. Tell us in the delivery notes.

## Returns

### What if something is not right?
Tell us within 24 hours of delivery and we will refund or replace it.
