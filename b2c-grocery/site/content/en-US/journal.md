## Small decisions

A good table is made of small decisions: the loaf you choose, the apples that were picked this week, the cheese from the farm down the road.

We spend our days visiting growers and bakers so that these decisions are easy for you.

## What we look for

Taste first, then care for the land, then a fair price for the person who made it.
