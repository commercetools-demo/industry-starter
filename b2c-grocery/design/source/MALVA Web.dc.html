Journal · No. 14

The quiet table

Words by Sofia Renaldi · Photographs, Ostuni

In a farmhouse outside Ostuni, four generations have thrown the same shape of bowl. We spent two days watching clay dry and came back with a table setting that refuses to shout.

The workshop has no showroom and no website. Orders arrive by telephone, are written in a ledger, and leave on a Thursday. What the family makes has barely changed since the sixties — a low bowl, a wide plate, a jug with a thumbprint where the handle meets the body — because nobody there believes the shapes need improving.

“A good object is one you stop noticing after a week, and miss instantly when it's gone.”

We chose five pieces for the table, and one for the light above it. Everything here is glazed in the same satin oat, so a setting can be built up slowly over years without ever needing to match.
